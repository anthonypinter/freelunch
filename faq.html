FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What are The Lunch Games?

The Lunch Games is a semester-long competition to see who can get the most free meals on campus. Inspired by the phrase “There’s no such thing as a free lunch,” competitors submit photos of their free meals along with a description of what they had to do to get that free meal.

Who created The Lunch Games?

The concept of The Lunch Games was conceived by the Humans of Whaaat!? during a lunchtime meeting (that we all paid for).

What does the winner of The Lunch Games get?

The winner of each semester’s Lunch Games will receive a customized silver lunch tray courtesy of our partners, the Society of Silver Saucers. The tray will commemorate the semester and the victor’s number of free meals.

Where does the phrase “There’s no such thing as a free lunch” come from?

It historically refers to the late 19th century practice of saloons giving patrons a free lunch if they bought a drink. Traditionally, the lunch provided was very salty as a way to encourage additional beverage purchases. More broadly, it is used societally as a way of acknowledging that one cannot get something for nothing.
